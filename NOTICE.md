# Third-party content

EuroCoins bundles content that is not covered by the MIT license of the source code.

## Coin images and descriptions

© European Central Bank / European Union — <https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/coins/>

The coin images, titles and descriptions in `src/EuroCoins/Assets/Catalog` are taken
from the ECB's directory of euro coins. The EU/ECB permits the reproduction of euro
coin images in flat format with a true-to-original depiction. Every coin in the
catalog stores its image credit and source page, and the app shows them in the
details pane. The images are reduced to at most 540 pixels per side.
